The fastest website projects have one thing in common: the client shows up prepared. Here's what to gather before kickoff.

## Brand assets

- **Logo files.** SVG or high-resolution PNG with a transparent background.
- **Colors and fonts.** If you have brand guidelines, send them.
- **Examples you like.** Two or three sites you admire, plus one you don't.

## Pages and copy

- **A page list.** Home, services, about, work, contact, and anything else you need.
- **Draft copy for each page.** Bullet points are fine. Focus on what you do, who it's for and why clients choose you.
- **Common questions.** The questions you answer on every sales call make great website content.

## Proof

- **Project examples.** Photos, case studies or before-and-after images.
- **Testimonials.** Short quotes from real clients, with their permission.
- **Credentials.** Certifications, awards or memberships worth mentioning.

## Photos

- **Your team.** Natural, well-lit portraits work better than stiff headshots.
- **Your space or work.** Wide shots and close-ups.
- **Originals.** Send full-resolution files, not screenshots.

## Access

- **Domain.** Login for your registrar or DNS provider.
- **Current website.** Access to the existing site, if there is one.
- **Connected tools.** Booking, email marketing or forms.

## Pull it together in one folder

Put everything in one shared folder, organized by page. It saves hours of back-and-forth and gets you to launch sooner.
