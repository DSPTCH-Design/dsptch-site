Website pricing can feel opaque because two quotes for "a website" can describe very different projects. Knowing what drives the cost makes it easier to compare options.

## What drives the price

**Page count.** Every page needs layout, content and testing. More pages, more time.

**Design customization.** A refined, art-directed design takes more care than adapting a template.

**Content.** If your copy and images are ready, the project moves faster. Copywriting, editing and photography are real work and are often priced separately.

**Functionality.** Contact forms are standard. Online stores, booking systems, member areas and custom integrations add significant scope.

**Revisions.** Clear, consolidated feedback rounds keep projects on schedule. Open-ended revisions are a common reason budgets grow.

## Why fixed-scope packages help

A fixed-scope package defines the page count, inclusions, revision rounds and exclusions before work begins. You know the price in advance, and both sides know what "done" means.

Our website packages work this way:

- **Refresh, $3,500:** up to 5 pages, one established design direction, a contact form and one revision round.
- **Elevate, $5,500:** up to 10 pages, a more refined design treatment, light copy editing, core technical SEO setup and two revision rounds.
- **Accelerate, $9,500:** up to 15 pages, a messaging workshop, selected copywriting, additional customization, analytics and a search-readiness review.

Final scope is always confirmed before work begins.

## How to compare quotes

Line up what each quote includes: pages, design approach, who writes the content, revision rounds, technical setup, timeline assumptions and what happens after launch. The cheapest quote is often the one that leaves the most work to you.
