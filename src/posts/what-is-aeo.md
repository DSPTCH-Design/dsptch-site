People increasingly ask AI assistants the questions they used to type into a search bar. Instead of ten blue links, they get a written answer, sometimes with a few sources attached. Answer engine optimization, or AEO, is about making your business one of the sources those answers can draw on.

## How AEO and SEO relate

Traditional SEO asks: will this page rank for this search? AEO asks a slightly different question: if someone asks a question this page can answer, is the answer easy to find, easy to understand and easy to trust?

In practice, the work overlaps. Both reward:

- **Clear structure.** Descriptive headings, short paragraphs and one topic per page.
- **Accurate information.** Business details, services and prices that match everywhere they appear.
- **Real expertise.** Content written by people who know the subject, not filler.

Where AEO differs is emphasis. It favors content that states the answer early and directly, then supports it.

## What answer-ready content looks like

An answer-ready page usually:

1. **Leads with the answer.** One or two sentences that directly respond to the question in the title.
2. **Uses the customer's words.** If clients ask "how long does a website take," that phrase belongs on the page.
3. **Covers the follow-up questions.** A short FAQ catches the next three things a reader wants to know.
4. **Uses structured data.** Markup such as FAQ and article schema helps machines read the page correctly.

## What not to do

- Don't publish thin pages built only around keywords.
- Don't hide important information in images or PDFs.
- Don't make claims you can't support. Trust is part of the job.

## Where to start

List the ten questions customers ask you most often, before they buy. Then check whether your website answers each one clearly. That gap list is your AEO plan.
