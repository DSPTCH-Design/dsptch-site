Privacy policy.

Last updated November 6, 2026

This policy explains what information DSPTCH Design ("DSPTCH," "we," "us") collects when you visit dsptchdesign.com or contact us through it, how we use that information and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you fill out a form on this site, such as Start a Project or a website inquiry, we collect the details you choose to share. That may include:

  • Your name and work email address
  • Your company, current website and type of business
  • Project details such as page count, timing, budget range and the kind of design work you need
  • Anything else you write in a message field

If you email us directly, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the time of your visit. This is used to keep the site running, secure and fast.

When you arrive from an ad or a link with tracking tags, the Start a Project form may record that source (for example, the campaign name) along with your submission, so we know which channels bring people to us.

This site does not currently use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy first.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and recommend a next step
  • To prepare proposals and deliver services you engage us for
  • To keep records of our conversations and agreements
  • To understand which pages and channels are working, in aggregate
  • To protect the site against spam and abuse

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for other companies' marketing.

Service providers we rely on

We use a small number of trusted providers to run this website:

  • Netlify hosts the website and processes form submissions on our behalf.
  • Google Fonts delivers the typeface used on the site. Your browser requests the font files from Google, which receives your IP address as part of that request.
  • Email and business tools we use to communicate with you and manage projects.

These providers may only use your information to provide their services to us.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry details for as long as needed to respond and to follow up on a potential project, and project records for as long as needed for the engagement and our legal and accounting obligations. You can ask us to delete your inquiry at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws.

Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection, and we limit access to inquiry information to the people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please don't send sensitive information such as passwords or financial details through our forms.

Children

This site is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our site or services change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
