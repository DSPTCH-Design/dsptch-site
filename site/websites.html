Questions
Can we update the website ourselves?

Editing options depend on the build and content setup. We explain the handoff before development starts, so you know exactly how updates will work.

What happens to our current content?

We review it with you during scoping. Packages assume client-approved copy; extensive content migration is scoped separately.

Who owns the finished site?

You own the agreed website assets and your domain. Hosting, third-party licenses and code handoff terms are defined in your agreement.

What if we need additional pages?

Additional pages and functionality are scoped and priced separately, before any work on them begins.
